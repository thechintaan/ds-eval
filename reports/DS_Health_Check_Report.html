Verdict

Production-ready — not fully self-contained.
Mode coverage and component depth are best-in-class. Nothing public comes close to 32 modes and 25 dedicated component pages. The gaps — spacing, effects, descriptions — are intentional architectural choices in M3's design: those layers live outside the Figma file in Google's external spec. Teams adopting M3 as a base need to add those two collections to make the file standalone.
